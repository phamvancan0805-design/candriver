☁ CanDriver

Lightweight Google Drive mount manager for Windows 10/11.

CanDriver is a small Windows desktop app that mounts your own Google Drive as a local drive letter (for example G:), using Rclone and WinFsp.

CanDriver là ứng dụng Windows nhỏ gọn giúp gắn Google Drive của chính bạn thành một ổ đĩa trên máy tính.

How it works

  • You sign in with Google through the standard OAuth consent screen. CanDriver never sees your password.
  • The access token is stored only on your own computer.
  • Files are read and written directly between your PC and Google Drive. There is no CanDriver server.
