CanDriver Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

What CanDriver is

CanDriver is a desktop application for Windows that runs entirely on your own computer. It lets you mount your own Google Drive as a local drive letter.

Google user data we access

With your consent, CanDriver requests the Google Drive scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) so it can list, read, create, modify and delete files in your Google Drive when you use the mounted drive. CanDriver only performs the file operations you initiate through Windows (for example opening, copying or deleting a file on the mounted drive).

How the data is used and stored

  • Your files travel directly between your computer and Google's servers. CanDriver has no server and the developer never receives your files or metadata.
  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on your computer, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\CanDriver.
  • CanDriver does not collect analytics, does not sell data, does not use your data for advertising, and does not transfer it to any third party.
  • Use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\CanDriver to remove the stored token, and revoke CanDriver's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
